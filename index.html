How it works

Three steps, zero setup. The host tab is the authority — no game server in the middle.

01

Create a room

Hit create and your browser becomes the host. No account, no install, no game server.

02

Share the link

Send the invite link to a friend. They land straight in your lobby, ready to play.

03

Race to five

Play a card, place a chip, draw a new one. Claim two sequences before your rival does.

What you get

🔒

Private rooms

Only people with the invite link can join your table.

⚡

Peer-to-peer

Moves travel directly between browsers over WebRTC.

🔁

Instant rematch

One click resets the board for another round.

🛡️

Fair play

The host validates every move before it reaches the board.

Hilera

Built with SolidStart + WebRTC. No game server, no tracking.
