About

Hilera is a two-player card-and-board game. Play a card, place a chip on a matching space, and draw a replacement. Five chips in a row make a sequence — claim two sequences and the game is yours.

How a turn works

A turn is one atomic move: the card you play, the chip you place (or remove with a one-eyed jack), and the mandatory draw all commit together. If a card has no legal use, you may discard it once per turn and draw a replacement without ending your turn.

Built for the browser

There is no game server. The hosting browser tab is the authority: it validates every move, keeps the deck and hands secret, and broadcasts only the public board state. Players connect directly over WebRTC, so a room is just a link.

  • SolidStart + SolidJS for the UI
  • PeerJS / WebRTC for peer-to-peer play
  • Tailwind CSS + daisyUI, restyled neobrutalist
