Privacy

Hilera has no accounts, no analytics, and no game server. This page explains the little data the game does touch, and where it goes.

What stays in your browser

Hilera stores a few values in sessionStorage — per-tab storage that disappears when you close the tab:

  • peerRole and peerRoomId — whether this tab is hosting or joining, and which room.
  • hostPeerId:<roomId> — the host's signaling id, so a reload can reclaim the same room.
  • game:<roomId> — the host tab's snapshot of the game (board, deck order, and both hands), so a reload can resume the exact game.

Nothing is written to cookies or localStorage, and nothing is sent to us. Leaving the room or closing the tab clears it.

Who else is involved

Three parties help deliver the game. None of them is us, and none receives your data from us:

  • The static host. This site is served by GitHub Pages, which may log requests (including IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The PeerJS signaling server. To introduce two browsers, the game uses the public PeerJS broker (0.peerjs.com by default). It sees your IP address, connection metadata, and the room id while a connection is being set up.
  • The other player. Once connected, moves travel directly between browsers over WebRTC. WebRTC can reveal your public IP address to the peer you are playing with, and the room id is in the invite link — anyone who has the link can join.

There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking pixels, and no third-party fonts or scripts: the fonts are served from this site.

Your rights

We do not run a server and do not store personal data, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. If you are in the EU or UK, the signaling server's processing is covered by PeerJS's own policy, and the direct peer connection is between you and the other player.

Questions or a takedown request? Open an issue on the project repository.

Children

Hilera is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
